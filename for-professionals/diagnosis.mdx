# The ADHD Diagnostic Process

## DSM-5 Criteria

A diagnosis of ADHD requires:

1. **Symptom count**: ≥6 inattentive symptoms OR ≥6 hyperactive-impulsive symptoms (≥5 for individuals 17 and older)
2. **Duration**: Symptoms present for at least 6 months
3. **Age of onset**: Several symptoms present before age 12
4. **Pervasiveness**: Symptoms present in two or more settings
5. **Impairment**: Clear evidence that symptoms interfere with functioning
6. **Exclusion**: Symptoms not better explained by another mental disorder

## Specifiers

- **314.01 (F90.2)** — Combined presentation
- **314.00 (F90.0)** — Predominantly inattentive presentation
- **314.01 (F90.1)** — Predominantly hyperactive-impulsive presentation
- **In partial remission** — when full criteria were previously met but fewer than required for the past 6 months

## Severity levels
- **Mild**: Few symptoms beyond threshold; minor functional impairment
- **Moderate**: Symptoms or impairment between mild and severe
- **Severe**: Many symptoms, marked impairment in multiple settings

## Comprehensive evaluation components

### For children
- Clinical interview with parents covering developmental, medical, family, and school history
- Interview with child (age-appropriate)
- Behavior rating scales from parents and teachers (e.g., Conners, BRIEF, BASC)
- Review of academic records and prior evaluations
- Medical examination
- Screening for coexisting conditions

### For adults
- Self-report rating scales (e.g., ASRS, CAARS)
- Clinical interview: current symptoms, childhood history, functional impairments
- Collateral report from partner, parent, or close colleague
- Review of records when available
- Neuropsychological testing (not required for diagnosis but may clarify profile)

## Differential diagnosis

Conditions that may mimic ADHD or coexist:

- Anxiety disorders (especially GAD)
- Depression and bipolar disorder
- Learning disabilities
- Sleep disorders
- Thyroid dysfunction
- Sensory impairments (hearing, vision)
- Autism spectrum disorder
- Post-traumatic stress disorder
- Substance use disorders

## Diagnosis in adults

Adult diagnosis requires retrospective confirmation of childhood onset. When records are unavailable:
- Old report cards, school records, or parent recall
- Patient self-report of childhood symptoms (with awareness of recall bias)
