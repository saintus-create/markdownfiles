# Diagnosis & Treatment of Children

## Getting an evaluation

A comprehensive evaluation for ADHD in children typically includes:

- Clinical interview with parents and child
- Behavior rating scales completed by parents and teachers
- Review of school performance records
- Medical examination to rule out other causes
- Assessment for coexisting conditions

**Who can evaluate?** Pediatricians, child psychiatrists, child psychologists, and developmental pediatricians.

## Treatment overview

### For children under age 6 (preschool)
- **Behavior therapy is the recommended first-line treatment** before medication
- Parent Training in Behavior Management (PTBM) is particularly effective
- Medication (methylphenidate) may be considered if behavior therapy does not provide sufficient improvement

### For children age 6 and older
- **Combined treatment** (medication + behavior therapy) produces the best outcomes
- Behavior therapy remains an important component even when medication is used

## Medication in ADHD treatment

| Medication type | Examples | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Stimulants (methylphenidate) | Ritalin, Concerta, Metadate | Most studied; effective for most children |
| Stimulants (amphetamine) | Adderall, Vyvanse, Dexedrine | Alternative to methylphenidate |
| Non-stimulants | Strattera, Intuniv, Qelbree | For those who cannot tolerate stimulants |

**Medications are not a cure** — they reduce symptoms while active and work best as part of a comprehensive treatment plan.

## Psychosocial treatments

- **Parent Training in Behavior Management** — teaches specific strategies for home
- **Behavioral classroom interventions** — teacher-implemented strategies at school
- **Social skills training** — helps children build peer relationships
- **Organization skills training** — particularly helpful for older children and teens

## Monitoring treatment

Treatment should be reviewed at least annually, or more frequently when:
- Your child is starting a new school year
- There are significant changes in symptoms or functioning
- Side effects are a concern
- Medication dosage may need adjustment due to growth
